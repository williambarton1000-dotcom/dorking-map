      Childminders are not pinned — the public Ofsted file redacts names and home addresses. As at 30 June 2026 there were 81 registered childminders in the Dorking and Horley constituency (wider than this map, because it includes Horley) and 866 in Surrey, plus one Surrey childminder without domestic premises. Search Surrey’s Childcare Finder or Ofsted inspection reports. There is also a “Find a local childminder” pin at Dorking Library.

      Sources

      Clubs and groups are taken from each organisation’s own website where one exists, or from a single reliable listing, checked on 22 November 2026. Schools come from the Department for Education’s Get Information About Schools extract of 22 November 2026. Nurseries and pre-schools come from Ofsted’s childcare providers file as at 30 June 2026. Both official datasets are published under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Nothing has been invented; if a time, age, rating or address was unclear it is marked as such or left blank. Price figures on the “Where to live” layer are area-level medians of standard (Category A) sales from HM Land Registry Price Paid Data for 2023–2025.
